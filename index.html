The Basics
Riding the T
Tap your credit card or phone at the reader — no ticket needed. Single ride is $2.40. Kids under 11 ride free. Runs roughly 5am–1am daily.

Pro Tips
"Tap, don't swipe."
Just tap your contactless card or Apple Pay at the gate — no app or ticket needed. Works on every line.
"The T stops at 1am."
Last trains are around 12:30–1am. Plan pub nights accordingly or budget for an Uber home.

Worth Knowing
Rose Kennedy Greenway
Already on the Wednesday route — beautiful parks, fountains, and open space, perfect for a 4-year-old to run around.
Charles River Esplanade
One of the nicest spots in the city for a relaxed afternoon — playgrounds, river views, and wide paths for walking.
Boston Public Market vs. Quincy Market
Worth prioritizing over Quincy Market for quality local food. Haymarket sits right next door for an easy combo stop.
View Boston Observation Deck (Optional, Paid)
If one paid attraction beyond the ballgames and Salem sounds appealing, this is the pick — panoramic views that pair well with Friday's visit to the Prudential Center.
